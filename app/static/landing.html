OUR VISION

The waiting room should be a choice, not a requirement.

We imagine neighbourhood service businesses where time is visible, capacity is coordinated, and every customer knows when to arrive. TrimTime starts with barber shops: a familiar place where a simple shared queue can make the entire day feel better.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

“TrimTime began with one straightforward question: why should a customer lose an hour just to hold a place in line?”

The product is being built around a practical promise—give customers their time back while helping local barber shops serve more calmly and consistently. Every feature, from the QR entry point to barber-level reporting, supports that promise.
