Dynamic Minute-by-Minute Chair Timers

When a barber starts a cut, elapsed time is calculated continuously. If a service takes 15 of 25 minutes, the system dynamically adjusts wait times for everyone outside.

Instant Storefront QR Code

Generate high-resolution printable QR codes for your window, front desk, and mirrors. Customers scan and follow their queue from anywhere with no app install.

Unified Walk-in & Appointment Queue

Same-day appointments merge seamlessly into the live line at their scheduled slot. Never double-book or spark arguments about who was first.
